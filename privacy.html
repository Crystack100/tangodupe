Purrzle privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Purrzle is a cat logic puzzle game. This page explains what information the game uses.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars, themes and settings are saved only on your device. We do not have accounts and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before using the advertising ID for tracking, and you can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice from Purrzle's settings under "Privacy choices".

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us.

Children

Purrzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions? Email the developer at the address listed on Purrzle's App Store or Google Play page.
