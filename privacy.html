Purrzle privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Purrzle is a cat logic puzzle game for iPhone, Android, the web and CrazyGames. This page explains what information the game uses.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars, themes and settings are saved only on your device. We do not have accounts and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes your progress.

On CrazyGames, your progress is saved through CrazyGames so it can follow your CrazyGames account. CrazyGames' own privacy policy covers that: crazygames.com/privacy-policy.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before using the advertising ID for tracking, and you can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice from Purrzle's settings under "Privacy choices".

On CrazyGames, ads are shown by CrazyGames and its ad partners, not by Purrzle. The web version at github.io shows no real ads.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the use of your information for personalized ads from Purrzle's settings under "Privacy choices" where that option is shown, or by turning off ad personalization in your device settings. We do not sell your information for money.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

In the phone app you can buy Purrzle Plus (a subscription) or Remove ads (a one-time purchase). Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; Purrzle never sees your card details. To check what you have bought and keep it working across reinstalls, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information. Learn more: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us.

Sharing your time

If you tap Share, your phone's share sheet opens with a short message about your solve time. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Purrzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used Purrzle, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email the developer at the address listed on Purrzle's App Store or Google Play page.
