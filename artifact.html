How to play
  • Tap a square to cycle sun, moon, then empty.
  • Each row and column holds 3 suns and 3 moons.
  • No more than 2 of the same symbol may sit next to each other.
  • Squares joined by = match. Squares joined by × are opposite.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you never need to guess.
